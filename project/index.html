Who We Are

Engineering Experience. Infrastructure for Tomorrow.

Logamalaikannan Government Contractor (KLK) is a civil contracting business based in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, with documented experience in government civil works, particularly water infrastructure, canal rehabilitation, anicuts, drainage structures, earthwork, and public facilities.

Our project portfolio reflects experience in both routine civil works and major infrastructure assignments. Building on this foundation, KLK aims to expand into highways, road infrastructure, buildings, and larger civil engineering projects.
