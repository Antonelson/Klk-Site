Who We Are

Built on precision, delivered on trust.

Logamalaikannan Government Contractor (KLK) is a proprietorship civil contracting business operating from Madurai, Tamil Nadu. The company has a documented portfolio of government civil works spanning water infrastructure, rehabilitation, drainage, earthwork, concrete structures, and public facilities.
With experience supported by government work certificates, project records, and execution documentation, KLK continues to build its civil engineering capabilities and pursue opportunities in larger infrastructure projects.
Our Vision
To build on our government civil-work experience and develop our capabilities in highways, buildings, and larger infrastructure projects.
Our Mission
To execute civil infrastructure works with a focus on sound engineering practices, systematic project planning, quality-conscious execution, and the development of durable public infrastructure.
